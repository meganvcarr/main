The opioid epidemic and drug overdose crisis continue to pose significant public health challenges in the United States. Nearly 108,000 people died from drug overdoses in 2022, with approximately 82,000 (76%) of those deaths involving opioids. Youth have experienced persistently elevated overdose death rates in recent years, emphasizing the urgent need for targeted prevention measures that expand access to overdose prevention resources like naloxone and fentanyl test strips.

Our Solution

Through education, resources, and community action, we're building a world where no life is lost to overdose.

Mission

End Overdose is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization committed to ending drug-related overdose deaths through education, medical intervention, and public awareness. We focus on equipping communities with training, naloxone, and test strips to prevent and respond to an overdose crisis.

Vision

No One Else Has To D!e. A world where no life is lost to overdose and everyone has the chance to access overdose education, prevention, and response resources.

Values

  1. 1.Culturally Relevant and Compassionate Messaging: We commit to delivering culturally relevant and compassionate messaging by tailoring its educational materials and outreach strategies using data-driven insights to resonate with young people
  2. 2.Naloxone Training and Accessibility: We commit to providing no-cost overdose prevention education, resources, and behavioral health support to ensure equitable access for all. Naloxone, test strips, and training can be obtained immediately and online– at the click of a button
  3. 3.Youth Empowerment: We recognize that equipping young individuals with knowledge and resources fosters informed decision-making, resilience, and proactive roles in preventing overdose within their communities
  4. 4.Peer-to-Peer Education & Community-Driven Action: With 500+ volunteers and 80+ chapters nationwide, we aim to foster peer-to-peer education and create expansive distribution networks to equip people with the tools, knowledge, and confidence to save lives. We are dedicated to pursuing forward-thinking, evidence-based interventions to address the overdose crisis
  5. 5.Fostering a Digital Community: We leverage social and digital media platforms to create compelling messaging campaigns that are culturally relevant and amplified by collaborations with social media influencers to raise awareness
  6. 6.Sustainable Initiatives + Community Partnerships: We believe in the power of partnerships with individuals, communities, and organizations to amplify our impact. Cross-industry collaboration with music, art, education, and governmental organizations produces innovative communication campaigns
