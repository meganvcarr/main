Our Solution

Prevent. Respond. Save a life.

Naloxone, test strips, and training can be obtained immediately and online. Our vision is a world where no life is lost to overdose.

Training

Free, 30-minute overdose prevention and response training — in person or online — for schools, workplaces, and communities.

Naloxone

Naloxone (Narcan) quickly restores breathing to a person whose breathing has slowed or stopped from an opioid overdose.
